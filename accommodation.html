Stay Locations

Recommended Accommodation Zones

Within 10–20 mins of Rajagiri Campus
01 Premium & Business Hotels

Edappally & Lulu Mall Zone

4.5 km from Campus 10–12 mins drive

Kochi's primary retail and commercial hub. Offers premium accommodation options with extensive dining, transit, and shopping connections at Lulu Mall and metro access.

Transit Recommendations Direct metro connection from Edappally Station to Kalamassery Town Station.
02 IT Corridor Business Hotels

Kakkanad & Infopark Zone

6.0 km from Campus 12–15 mins drive

The tech hub of Kochi. Features executive business hotels, boutique residencies, and serviced apartments suitable for researchers and corporate delegates.

Transit Recommendations Direct taxi, auto-rickshaw, or campus shuttle arrangements.
03 Convenient Transit Stays

Kalamassery & Aluva Highway

1.5 km from Campus 3–5 mins drive

Budget-friendly options, student residencies, and guest houses located directly along NH 544 and within walking distance of Kalamassery Town Metro Station.

Transit Recommendations Short walking distance (200m) or rapid 3-minute auto-rickshaw ride.
