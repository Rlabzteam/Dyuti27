# Travel Connectivity Guide

## Airport Connectivity
- **Cochin International Airport (COK)**: 22 km (25–35 min taxi drive via NH 544).

## Metro Connectivity
- **Kalamassery Town Metro Station**: 200m from Rajagiri campus (3-min walk).

## Railway Stations
- **Aluva Railway Station (AWY)**: 6 km.
- **Ernakulam South (ERS) & North (ERN)**: 12–14 km.
