# Frequently Asked Questions

### Q: Where is DYUTI 2027 hosted?
Rajagiri College of Social Sciences (Autonomous), Kalamassery, Kochi, Kerala, India.

### Q: How do I submit an abstract?
Abstract submissions are handled via the Microsoft CMT portal. Links are published on the Call for Papers page.

### Q: Are proceedings peer-reviewed?
Yes, all submitted papers undergo double-blind peer review.
